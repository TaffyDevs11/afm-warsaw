Contact & social links

Type them however you have them — @handle, a full link, or a number with spaces. Each one becomes a working button (WhatsApp opens a chat, Call dials, Instagram opens the profile). Leave any field empty to hide that button.

A 9-digit WhatsApp number without a country code is treated as Polish (+48).

Logo

Square or transparent PNG works best. Without one, the business initials are shown.

Flyers & posters

Upload as many adverts, flyers or posters as the business has. They appear on the business page in a gallery; visitors can open each one full size and page through them.

JPG, PNG, WEBP or GIF, up to 12MB each. Portrait posters and square social-media flyers both display well.
